Gmail keyboard shortcuts: the cheat sheet.

Learn twelve keys and you will never reach for the mouse to clear your inbox again. Here is how to turn shortcuts on, the twelve that matter most, and the full list.

By Sai Veda · Updated November 5, 2026

Turn them on first

  1. In Gmail on the web, click the gear and choose See all settings.
  2. On the General tab, find Keyboard shortcuts and select Keyboard shortcuts on.
  3. Scroll down and click Save Changes.

Press ? in Gmail at any time to see the list. Shortcuts are ignored while you are typing in a box; press Esc first.

Questions

How do I turn on Gmail keyboard shortcuts?

In Gmail on the web, open Settings (the gear), choose See all settings, find Keyboard shortcuts on the General tab, select Keyboard shortcuts on, and click Save Changes at the bottom.

What is the Gmail shortcut to archive an email?

Press E to archive the open or selected conversation. Press Z right after to undo.

Why are my Gmail shortcuts not working?

Single-key shortcuts only work after you turn them on in Settings, and they are ignored while the cursor is in a text box such as the search bar or a reply. Press Esc to leave the text box first.

Are there shortcuts for snoozing in Gmail?

Yes. Press B to snooze the selected conversation.
