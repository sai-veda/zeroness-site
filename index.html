Email, at the speed of thought.

Zeroness is a fast, AI-native email client for Gmail. It screens your inbox, drafts replies in your voice when someone is waiting on you, and puts every action a keystroke away.

Screened for you

Important mail, your team, calendar invites, newsletters and receipts each get their own place, so the few threads that need you rise to the top.

Drafts when they matter

When a real person is waiting on you, Zeroness writes the reply from the whole conversation and what you have already decided. You review it and press Send.

Built for the keyboard

E for done, H to come back later, ⌘K to just ask. Actions apply instantly and sync to Gmail behind you.

How Zeroness uses your Google data

Zeroness connects to Gmail with your permission to show, search, organize, draft and send your email inside the app. Your address, mail and labels stay in Google, and everything you do in Zeroness shows up in Gmail too. Zeroness never sends mail without your action.

Zeroness's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Your data is never sold, never used for advertising and never used to train AI models. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Availability

Zeroness for web and iPhone is in early access for Gmail and Google Workspace. Pricing: $9.99 a month or $30 a year at launch, free for work email during launch, with a student discount.
