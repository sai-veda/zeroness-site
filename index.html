Do I need a new email address?

No. Zeroness works with the Gmail or Google Workspace address you already use. Your mail and labels stay in your account, and anything you do in Zeroness shows up everywhere else you read mail.

Will it send anything on its own?

Never. It writes drafts for threads where someone is waiting on you. You read them, change what you like, and press Send.

What happens to mail it clears?

It moves to Done, the same as archiving. Nothing is deleted, and Done shows what Zeroness cleared with one click to bring it all back.

Outlook?

Gmail and Google Workspace first. Outlook is next.
