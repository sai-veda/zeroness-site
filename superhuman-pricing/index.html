Superhuman pricing in 2026.

Questions

How much does Superhuman cost per month?

Superhuman Mail Starter is $30 a month billed monthly or $25 a month billed annually. Business is $40 monthly or $33 annually. Enterprise pricing is by quote. Prices are per member and checked November 5, 2026.

Is there a free version of Superhuman?

There is no free Superhuman Mail plan. The free Superhuman suite plan covers Grammarly, Docs and Go, not the Mail app.

Which Superhuman plan includes AI drafts?

Auto Drafts, Ask AI, and voice and tone matching are on Business and Enterprise, not Starter.

What changed after Grammarly bought Superhuman?

Grammarly acquired Superhuman Mail and Coda in 2025 and renamed the company Superhuman. The new suite bundles Mail with Grammarly, Docs and Go, and only includes Mail from the Business plan ($33 annual, $40 monthly). The standalone Mail Starter plan is still listed on the Mail plans page.
