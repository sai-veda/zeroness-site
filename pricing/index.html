Questions

What is the Founding 100?

The first 100 people to connect an account get Zeroness free until January 31, 2027, then $5 a month for as long as they stay subscribed, half the Personal price. Google limits apps that have not finished its security review to 100 users, so these are the only spots before that review.

How much does Zeroness cost?

Zeroness is free for work email on company domains during launch. Personal is $9.99 a month, or $30 for the first year at launch. Students pay $1 a month with a .edu address.

Is AI included?

Yes. Every plan includes every feature, including drafts, summaries, Ask and sorting.

Will my price go up after launch?

No. Launch prices stay the same for as long as you stay subscribed.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel any time and keep access until the end of the billing period.
