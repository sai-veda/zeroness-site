Choose Zeroness if

  • You use Gmail or Google Workspace and want Superhuman’s speed for a third of the price.
  • You want AI that does more than write: sort, clear finished threads, and act on plain-English requests.
  • You get a lot of vendor, investor or customer mail where an AI draft has to know what you already decided.

Choose Superhuman if

  • You need Outlook, Android or a desktop app today.
  • Your team relies on shared comments, read statuses or a calendar inside the email app.

Switching

There is nothing to migrate. Zeroness connects to your existing Gmail or Google Workspace account, and your labels, archive and sent mail stay exactly where they are. You can use both apps side by side while you decide.

Questions

Is Zeroness a Superhuman alternative?

Yes. Zeroness covers the core Superhuman workflow for Gmail and Google Workspace (split inbox, keyboard shortcuts, reminders, AI drafts) at $9.99 a month, with AI included on every plan and free work email during launch.

How much cheaper is Zeroness than Superhuman?

Zeroness Personal is $9.99 a month, or $30 for the first year at launch. Superhuman lists Starter at $25 and Business at $33 per member a month billed annually (about $30 and $40 billed monthly) as of November 2026.

What does Superhuman have that Zeroness does not?

As of November 2026, Superhuman supports Outlook, read statuses, team comments and a built-in calendar. Zeroness supports Gmail and Google Workspace, with Outlook and calendar planned.
