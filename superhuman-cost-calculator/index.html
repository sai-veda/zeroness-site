Questions

How much does Superhuman cost for a team?

Superhuman Mail lists Starter at $25 and Business at $33 per member a month billed annually (about $30 and $40 billed monthly) as of November 2026. A five-person team on Starter billed annually pays $1,500 a year.

How much does Zeroness cost for a team?

Zeroness is free for company domains on Google Workspace during launch. Personal accounts are $9.99 a month. The first 100 users get it free until January 31, 2027, then $5 a month for life.
