Questions

Is Zeroness a Notion Mail alternative?

Yes. Notion Mail shut down on September 22, 2026. Zeroness is an email app for Gmail and Google Workspace with an AI-sorted inbox, keyboard commands and drafted replies, on web and iPhone.

Do I need to migrate anything from Notion Mail?

No. Notion Mail synced with Gmail, so your mail is already in Gmail. Zeroness connects to the same account and sorts it on its own, with no rules to rebuild.

How much does Zeroness cost?

The first 100 users get it free until January 31, 2027, then $5 a month for life. After that, Personal is $9.99 a month and work email is free during launch.
