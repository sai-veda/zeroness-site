The short answer: for Gmail users, Zeroness gives you Superhuman's keyboard-first speed and AI drafts for $9.99 a month. If you need Outlook or Android, look at Spark or Missive. If you want to stay in Gmail itself, Gemini covers the basics for free.

Why people leave Superhuman

Price, mostly. Superhuman Mail is $25 a month per person billed annually, and the AI that writes replies for you (Auto Drafts) and answers questions about your mail (Ask AI) is only on Business, $33 a month annually or $40 monthly. Since the Grammarly acquisition, the Superhuman suite only includes Mail from Business up. For a three-person startup that is $1,188 to $1,440 a year for email.

Zeroness

An email app for Gmail and Google Workspace on web and iPhone. AI sorts every thread into Important, Team, Calendar, News and Other, drafts replies in your voice after checking your other mail for decisions already made, and every action has one key. Every feature is on every plan. Not yet: Outlook, Android, Mac and Windows apps, shared team inboxes. Zeroness vs Superhuman in detail.

Shortwave

The closest Gmail-only alternative, built around an AI agent you prompt to search, organize and write. Strong AI search across years of mail. Paid plans are billed monthly and the higher tiers are priced per seat. Fully automatic drafting runs through its sister product, Tasklet.

Spark

A long-standing cross-platform client with a capable free plan and support for almost any provider, including IMAP. AI auto-drafts and Autopilot are on Pro. We did not find a browser version.

Inbox Zero

Open source and self-hostable, with a free email app on web, desktop and iPhone and paid AI plans for categorizing, drafting and a cold-email blocker.

Fyxer AI

An assistant that works inside Gmail and Outlook rather than replacing them: drafts in your tone, labels and meeting notes, metered in credits. Sorting starts on Pro.

Missive

Built for teams: shared inboxes, comments and assignment across Gmail, Outlook and IMAP, with AI on every plan through credits or your own API key.

Gmail with Gemini

Gmail now summarizes threads and drafts from a prompt for free. AI inbox triage needs a paid Google AI plan and is a US, English-only beta. No tier writes drafts before you ask.

Questions

What is the cheapest Superhuman alternative?

For a full email app with AI drafts, Zeroness is $9.99 a month with AI on every plan (free for work email during launch). Spark has a free plan but puts auto-drafts on Pro at $20 a month. Gmail itself has free summaries and prompt-based drafting through Gemini.

Which Superhuman alternative works only with Gmail?

Zeroness and Shortwave both focus on Gmail and Google Workspace. Spark, Missive, Fyxer and Inbox Zero also support Outlook.

Does Superhuman's cheapest plan include AI drafts?

No. As of November 2026 Superhuman's Starter plan ($25 a month billed annually, $30 monthly) does not include Auto Drafts or Ask AI; both are on Business ($33 annual, $40 monthly).

What replaced Notion Mail?

Notion Mail shut down on September 22, 2026 and Notion did not launch a replacement inbox. Former users moved to Gmail apps such as Zeroness, Shortwave and Spark.
